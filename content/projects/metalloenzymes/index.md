### Doctoral Research at Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (InSiliChem Group)

This research project explores computational strategies for the rational design of artificial metalloenzymes and de novo metallopeptides:

* **Mechanistic Modelling:** Investigating gold-catalysed alkenynone cyclisation mechanisms within protein scaffolds using cluster DFT calculations (Gaussian 16).
* **De Novo Metallopeptide Design:** Computational design and structural characterisation targeting biocatalysis and drug discovery.
* **Molecular Dynamics & Structure Prediction:** Integrating AMBER molecular dynamics, AlphaFold prediction models, and custom Python trajectory analysis workflows.
