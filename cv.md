## Profile Summary
Chemist and PhD candidate in Computational Chemistry at the Universitat Autónoma de Barcelona (InSiliChem group). My doctoral research focuses on the rational design of artificial metalloenzymes and de novo metallopeptides for biocatalysis and drug design through molecular dynamics, structure prediction, and mechanistic modelling. I develop open-source Python tools to automate and deepen the analysis of simulations, integrating machine learning and AI models into these pipelines. My background also spans analytical method validation in the pharmaceutical industry, transcriptomics, neuroscience research, teaching, and science outreach.

## Research Software Developed
### Gephyra - Water-bridge network analysis for molecular dynamics
* **Year:** 2026 | **Authors:** Peralta-Morales, M. F., Sciortino, G. & Maréchal, J.-D. | [github.com/insilichem/Gephyra](https://github.com/insilichem/Gephyra)
* Pip-installable Python package with a command-line interface to discover, track and statistically analyse water-mediated hydrogen-bond networks in MD trajectories.
* Probabilistic hydrogen-bond scoring, weighted graph path search (NetworkX) and Fréchet-distance hierarchical clustering of pathways; exports visualisation scripts for PyMOL, VMD and UCSF Chimera.

### Water Counting - Water molecules per plane in UCSF Chimera
* **Year:** 2026 | **Authors:** Peralta-Morales, M. F., Sciortino, G. & Maréchal, J.-D. | [github.com/insilichem/Water-count-per-plane](https://github.com/insilichem/Water-count-per-plane)
* Python script for UCSF Chimera that counts water molecules across defined planes of a molecular system.
